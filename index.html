About

Based out of St. Louis, The Honey Creepers are pushing for a sort of sonic renaissance. Classically trained and a multi-instrumentalist, evolving, original compositions are birthed within a tiny studio apartment.

Blues and Americana roots are combined with Baroque pop and psychedelia of the 1960s to create a raw, gritty, listening experience. If you're a fan of thematic album rock, look no further, The Honey Creepers are creating music pulled from that iconic era today.
